Government Ministries

Departments of the Treekian Government

Overview

The ministries of the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics are responsible for the administration of national affairs and the implementation of government policy. Each ministry is led by an appointed official responsible for their respective portfolio.

Government Departments

Ministry of Foreign Affairs

Minister: General Christian

Responsible for diplomatic relations, international communications and foreign policy.

Ministry of Truth Speaking

Minister: General Manu the Great

Responsible for official government communications and public information.

National Nuclear Programme

Director: Noah

Responsible for research and administration of the national nuclear programme.

Committee of State Security

Chairman: Braedyn

Responsible for internal security, intelligence and the protection of national interests.

Mission

The ministries work together to ensure the effective administration of the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics, maintaining national stability, supporting government policy and serving the interests of the nation.
