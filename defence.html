Treekian Armed Forces

Defending the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics

Overview

The Treekian Armed Forces are responsible for the defence of the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics. The Armed Forces consist of the Army, Air Force and Navy, operating under the authority of the President as Commander-in-Chief.

Mission Statement

The mission of the Treekian Armed Forces is to protect the sovereignty, interests and security of the Treekian Union through disciplined military service, strategic readiness and national defence.
