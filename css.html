Committee of State Security

Security • Intelligence • National Protection

Overview

The Committee of State Security (CSS) is the national security and intelligence organisation of the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics. It is responsible for internal security, counterintelligence, and the protection of national interests.

Leadership

Chairman Commander
Branch State Security
Reports To President William
Status Active

Divisions

Internal Security

Maintains domestic stability and monitors internal threats.

Counterintelligence

Prevents and investigates foreign intelligence activity.

Cyber Operations

Handles digital security and information protection.

Protective Service

Provides protection for high-ranking government officials.

Mission Statement

To safeguard the sovereignty, stability, and security of the Treekian Union through intelligence gathering, internal protection, and strategic national security operations.
