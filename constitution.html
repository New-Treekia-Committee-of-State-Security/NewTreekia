Constitution of Treekia

Law • Structure • National Principles

Preamble

We, the people of the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics, in order to form a more perfect union, establish justice, ensure national stability, provide for the common defence, and secure the blessings of liberty and order, do hereby establish this Constitution for the governance of our nation.

Article I — The State

The Treekian Union of Socialist Republics is a sovereign socialist state governed under a centralized system of administration. All authority is derived from the national structure established by this Constitution.

Article II — The President

The President is the Head of State and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces. The President holds executive authority and oversees national governance, appointments, and state direction.

Article III — Government Structure

The Government shall consist of ministries, armed forces, and administrative bodies, each responsible for their designated national functions under presidential oversight.

Article IV — Armed Forces

The Treekian Armed Forces are responsible for the defence of the nation and consist of the Army, Air Force, and Navy, operating under unified command.

Article V — State Security

The Committee of State Security (CSS) is responsible for internal security, intelligence, and the protection of national sovereignty.

Article VI — National Identity

The national symbols, language, currency, and cultural identity of Treekia are protected under this Constitution and represent the unity of the state.

Article VII — Amendments

This Constitution may be amended by directive of the President or through formal governmental restructuring approved by national authority.

Conclusion

This Constitution serves as the foundational legal document of the Treekian Union of Socialist Republics and defines the principles upon which the nation is governed.

Committee of State Security
